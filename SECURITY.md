# Security Policy

## Scope

LASP is a local browser proof of concept and synthetic research laboratory. The repository is not a production security boundary, identity provider, payment system or compliance product. Reports are still welcome when they reveal a reproducible security defect, misleading security claim, unsafe demo behavior or a problem in the research boundaries.

## Supported version

The default branch is the actively reviewed version. Tagged releases are historical snapshots. No production SLA is offered.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not publish sensitive details in a public issue. Contact:

**Kava140982@gmail.com**

Include the affected commit or file, a minimal reproduction, impact, and whether the issue is confined to the synthetic demo or could affect a future implementation. Do not include real credentials, personal data or operational secrets.

## Response expectations

This is a research project maintained on a best-effort basis. A report may be acknowledged, reproduced in an isolated environment, documented as `observed`/`implemented`/`not_executed`, fixed, or closed as out of scope. A report does not imply a certification, bounty, service-level commitment or admission of liability.

## Disclosure

Please allow a reasonable coordination window before public disclosure when a finding could mislead users or affect downstream copies. The project may credit a reporter only with permission.
